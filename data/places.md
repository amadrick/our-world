## Andytown Coffee Roasters

Coffee · Outer Sunset · 3655 Lawton St, San Francisco, CA 94122

**Known for:** Snowy Plover (espresso cream soda) (high confidence). Official Andytown identity and Yelp: espresso, sparkling water, brown sugar, whipped cream—the Outer Sunset signature bird drink.

### Place research

- **Neighborhood and street:** Outer Sunset. Corner storefront at Lawton Street and 43rd Avenue on a quiet, mostly residential stretch of Lawton in the Outer Sunset, a few blocks inland from Ocean Beach. Since a 2022-23 remodel the cafe spans its original narrow unit plus the former shop next door.
- **Terrain and setting:** Gentle westward slope: from the corner you look down Lawton Street to a glimpse of the Pacific. Squarely in the Outer Sunset fog belt, often cold, gray and misty, with a sunny west-facing side wall and strong sunsets on clear evenings.
- **Architecture:** Modest neighborhood storefront, converted in 2014 from an earlier coffee shop into a tiny, long 600-sq-ft room with a big front-window seating area and a storage loft; the owners added windows for light. A Studio BANAA remodel absorbed the neighboring unit and nearly doubled the space, reopening around September 2023. Pre-remodel descriptions note clean white walls, succulents and rustic wood, with waiting benches inside and small tables on the sidewalk outside.
- **Unique architectural notes:** A 10-foot plywood red letter "A" salvaged from the demolished Andersonstown Leisure Centre in West Belfast hangs illuminated from the ceiling, its tip pointed toward Andersonstown.
- **Most iconic physical characteristics:**
  - illuminated 10-foot red "A" sign hung from the ceiling
  - corner storefront with big front windows at Lawton and 43rd
  - benches and small tables on the sidewalk outside
  - view down Lawton Street toward the Pacific
- **More recognizable view:** the interior
- **Not verified:** Facade color, exterior signage, building era/story count and post-remodel interior finishes not verified (white walls and rustic wood are pre-remodel descriptions). No source supports the older brief's pale blue-green front. Cross street: owners (Sprudge) and Avital say 43rd Ave; Time Out lists 42nd Ave.
