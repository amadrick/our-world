Create a **wide map underlay of San Francisco** for a contemporary recommendations app.

Show only the map itself. **No UI, cards, pins, markers, controls, photography, restaurant imagery, text panels, or interface elements.**

The aesthetic should combine a **modern, precise digital map with the softness and material character of Kodak 35mm film**.

Keep the actual map clean and legible:
- recognizable San Francisco geography
- fine street network
- major parks and green spaces
- coastline and bay
- subtle neighborhood structure
- restrained geographic labels if useful
- enough detail to feel like a real functional map, but never visually busy

### Visual direction

The base should be a **soft warm porcelain / pale stone**, not yellowed paper or vintage cream.

Land should feel warm-neutral and almost luminous.

Water should be a **soft but clearly noticeable desaturated blue** — cool enough to create contrast with the land, but restrained and sophisticated. Think dusty mineral blue, pale slate blue, or washed denim rather than cyan or baby blue.

Parks and natural areas should use extremely muted gray-green tones.

Streets should be fine, understated warm-gray lines with subtle hierarchy. Major roads may be slightly stronger, but avoid conventional colorful navigation-map styling.

Introduce a very subtle **analog photographic softness**:
- faint Kodak-like film grain
- extremely gentle optical blur
- slightly softened edges
- minute color variation
- subtle tonal unevenness
- very slight atmospheric haze

The effect should be perceptible rather than decorative. The map should still feel **clean, digital, precise, and current**.

Allow extremely subtle areas of diffuse light or blur near the edges, as though photographed through high-quality glass, but **almost no lens flare**. No large orange blooms, light leaks, sun streaks, glowing corners, or vintage-film effects.

The map needs to work underneath **modern translucent / Liquid Glass UI**, so keep the center visually restrained and preserve large calm areas where floating glass surfaces can sit without fighting the background.

Overall feeling:

**bleeding-edge interface foundation + soft analog color science**

Contemporary, quiet, spatial, tactile, slightly dreamy, but not nostalgic.

Avoid:
- antique map styling
- parchment
- sepia
- retro cartography
- distressed paper
- heavy grain
- film borders
- light leaks
- obvious lens flare
- orange haze
- excessive beige
- watercolor
- illustration
- hand-drawn streets
- exaggerated terrain
- saturated blue water
- Apple Maps imitation
- Google Maps imitation
- UI elements of any kind

The final image should feel like **a sophisticated modern map that has been gently passed through the color and optical softness of 35mm film**, rather than an old map or a photograph of a map.
